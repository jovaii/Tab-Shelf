# App Store Submission Checklist

Do not record membership, agreement, bank, tax, identifier, or credential values in this file. Verify each item in its account-owned system and record only completion status here.

- [ ] Confirm active Apple Developer Program membership.
- [ ] Confirm the Paid Apps Agreement is accepted.
- [ ] Confirm banking status is complete.
- [ ] Confirm tax status is complete.
- [ ] Confirm the App record is verified.
- [ ] Confirm the App and extension identifiers are verified.
- [ ] Confirm the USD 9.99 one-time purchase tier is selected.
- [ ] Confirm the release archive is validated.
- [ ] Confirm final screenshots are uploaded.
- [ ] Confirm privacy answers match the final candidate and its no-network and dependency audit.
- [ ] Confirm review notes are entered exactly as reviewed.
- [ ] Obtain explicit manual approval before selecting Submit for Review.
